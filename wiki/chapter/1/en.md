## Summary
After being surrounded by countless enemies, [Fang Yuan](/wiki/character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) is forced to use the [Spring Autumn Cicada](/wiki/gu/Spring_Autumn_Cicada/en.md) to go back five hundred years in time.
Back in time, the higher ups of the [Gu Yue clan](/wiki/organisation/Gu_Yue_clan/en.md) were holding a ceremony in the clan's main pavilion.
It was the day before the annual grand ceremony. The ceremony was held in hopes for said grand ceremony to bring great talents to the clan.
After the ceremony concluded, they discussed their worries about [Bai Ning Bing](/wiki/character/Bai_Ning_Bing/en.md) and their hopes for [Fang Yuan](/wiki/character/Fang_Yuan/en.md).
