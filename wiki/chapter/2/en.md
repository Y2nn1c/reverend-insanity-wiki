## Summary
After recalling the various rumours surrounding the [Spring Autumn Cicada](../../gu/Spring_Autumn_Cicada/en.md) and realising that it didn't come with him back in time, [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) started to think about how to use his five hundred years of knowledge. He got interrupted by his little twin brother [Fang Zheng](../../character/Fang_Zheng/en.md) who wished to close his window in worry of [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) getting sick. After thinking back to his previous life where he and his brother were taken in by his [uncle](../../character/Fang_Uncle/en.md) and [aunt](../../character/Fang_Aunt/en.md), so that they could claim their parents' inheritance for themselves, he tells [Fang Zheng](../../character/Fang_Zheng/en.md) that he can take his leave, which he does. He also remembered how cold he and his brother were treated by his [uncle](../../character/Fang_Uncle/en.md) and [aunt](../../character/Fang_Aunt/en.md), until [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) started to come up with poems, which he knew from his previous life from earth. Thanks to this, he and his brother started to get treated better, however [Fang Zheng](../../character/Fang_Zheng/en.md) never understood this and just thought that his brother was a genius, who he felt was somebody he couldn't compare with. At the [awakening ceremony](../../organisation/Gu_Yue_clan/en.md#grand-ceremony), it turned out that [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) was only a C grade talent and [Fang Zheng](../../character/Fang_Zheng/en.md) turned out to have A grade talent, which he hated for years to come. He then formulated his plan to reach rank three in order to be able to leave the [mountain](../../location/Qing_Mao_mountain/en.md).
