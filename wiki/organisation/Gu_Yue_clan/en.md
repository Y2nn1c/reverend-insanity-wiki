The Gu Yue clan (的古月山寨, Gu Yue mountain village, ancient moon mountain village) is a clan located on [Qing Mao mountain](../../location/Qing_Mao_mountain/en.md).

# Grand ceremony
The annual grand ceremony which is held each spring.

# Locations
## Main Pavilion
A magnificent pavilion in the middle of the clan which houses ceremonies. 
The floor is yellow brownish.
It holds a tall, black laquered table with three layers on it's case, each housing an ancestral tablet.
Copper incense is situated on both sides of the tablets.
One of them is a prayer by the clan's higher ups, in hopes for great talents at the annual grand ceremony.
