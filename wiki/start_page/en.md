# Welcome to the Reverend Insanity wiki

Reverend Insanity is a webnovel written by Hu YongXiang, who is best known under his pen name Gu Zhen Ren.<br>
The novel was released in 2012 on the chinese platform Qidian, where it became Gu Zhen Ren's most popular work.<br>
It amassed a total of 2334 chapters until it's ban in 2019.

The english translation was published by Webnovel, which later discontinued and deleted it.

# Manhua
The novel received two manhua adaptations.<br>
The first was started in 2017 and later discontinued at 96 chapters.<br>
The second adaptation was started in 2025 as a fan project and is currently still ongoing.
