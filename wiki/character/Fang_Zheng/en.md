Gu Yue Fang Zheng (古月方正, ancient moon Fang Zheng, ancient moon upright)is the younger twin brother of Fang Yuan.^[2]

# Plot
He first appears when he tries to close the window of his brother's room out of worry, that [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md) might get sick. After being told to leave, he says goodbye and leaves [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md)'s room.^[2]
