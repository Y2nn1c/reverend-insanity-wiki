The uncle(舅父) of [Fang Yuan](../Fang_Yuan/en.md)^[1] and [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md)^[2].
After their parents died, he and [his wife](../Fang_Aunt/en.md) took [Fang Yuan](../Fang_Yuan/en.md)^[1] and [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md)^[2] in.^[1]
Both were three years old when they were adopted.^[2]
