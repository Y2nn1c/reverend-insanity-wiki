Gu Yue Fang Yuan (古月方源, ancient moon Fang Yuan) is the protagonist of Reverend Insanity.
Originally a chinese student from earth, he was transported into the gu world where he was born as part of the [Gu Yue clan](../../organisation/Gu_Yue_clan/en.md).
His first life in the gu world went on for five hundred years where he became a part of the demonic path before he was forced to use the [Spring Autumn Cicada](../../gu/Spring_Autumn_Cicada/en.md) to go back in time.

# Appearance
## Before traveling five hundred years back in time
He wore torn, green robes with disheveled hair while being drenched in blood from his numerous wounds.
His facial expression was completely calm with his eyes appearing like a bottomless well.^[1]

# Plot
After being forced to go back five hundred years in time with the [Spring Autumn Cicada](../../gu/Spring_Autumn_Cicada/en.md), Fang Yuan found himself back in the [Gu Yue clan](../../organisation/Gu_Yue_clan/en.md) on the day before the grand ceremony.^[1] Thinking about how to use his knowledge from his previous life, he gets interrupted by his younger twin brother [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md), who tells him that he wants to close his window, so that Fang Yuan doesn't get sick. He thought back to his previous life, where his [aunt](../Fang_Aunt/en.md) and [uncle](../Fang_Uncle/en.md) took Fang Yuan and [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md) in, in order to get their parents' inheritance. About how he pretended to come up with poems, which he copied from earth so that he and his brother woudln't be treated coldy by them anymore, without his brother ever understanding that he was just helping him. He then tells [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md) to take his leave.^[2]
