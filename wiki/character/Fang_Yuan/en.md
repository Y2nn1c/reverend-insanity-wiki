Gu Yue Fang Yuan (古月方源, ancient moon Fang Yuan) is the protagonist of Reverend Insanity.
Originally a chinese student from earth, he was transported into the gu world where he was born as part of the [Gu Yue clan](../../organisation/Gu_Yue_clan/en.md).
His first life in the gu world went on for five hundred years where he became a part of the demonic path before he was forced to use the [Spring Autumn Cicada](../../gu/Spring_Autumn_Cicada/en.md) to go back in time.

# Appearance
## Before traveling five hundred years back in time
He wore torn, green robes with disheveled hair while being drenched in blood from his numerous wounds.
His facial expression was completely calm with his eyes appearing like a bottomless well.
