The aunt(舅母) of [Fang Yuan](../Fang_Yuan/en.md)^[1] and [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md)^[2].
After their parents died, she and [her husband](../Fang_Uncle/en.md) took [Fang Yuan](../Fang_Yuan/en.md)^[1] and [Fang Zheng](../Fang_Zheng/en.md)^[2] in.^[1]
Both were three years old when they were adopted.^[2]
