Gu Yue Fang Yuan (古月方源, ancient moon Fang Yuan) is the protagonist of Reverend Insanity.
Originally a chinese student from earth, he was transported into the gu world where he was born as part of the Gu Yue clan.
His first life in the gu world went on for 500 years before he was forced to use the Spring Autumn Cicada to go back in time.
The clan higher ups hoped for him to be an A grade talent, at least B grade.

# Appearance
## Before traveling five hundred years back in time
He wore torn, green robes with disheveled hair while being drenched in blood from his numerous wounds.
His facial expression was completely calm with his eyes appearing like a bottomless well.

After being forced to go back five hundred years in time with the Spring Autumn Cicada, Fang Yuan found himself back in the Gu Yue clan on the day before the grand ceremony.
