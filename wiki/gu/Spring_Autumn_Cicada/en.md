The Spring Autumn Cicada (出春秋蟬) is the signature gu worm of [Fang Yuan](../../character/Fang_Yuan/en.md).
It was used to transport him five hundred years back into the past at the beginning of the novel.

# Ability
It takes the user and itself back in time^[1] by traveling the [River of Time](../../secluded_domain_of_heaven_and_earth/River_of_time/en.md) upstream^[2].^[1] The person's body and cultivation will be used as fuel.^[2] The user will wake up in their old body and retain all of their memories.^[1] The Spring Autumn Cicada stays as their refined gu.
